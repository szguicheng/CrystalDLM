## Scope and method

Reviewed all 30 rows of `pilot30-annotations-v2.jsonl`, immutable v3 raw receipts in `pilot30-annotations-v3.jsonl`, the legacy `pilot30-rendered.jsonl`, and final `pilot30-rendered-final.jsonl` against `pilot30-facts-v2.jsonl`. The exact request payloads are stored in the receipt JSONL records. No API, `.env`, network, or remote state was accessed; local feature-method documentation and annotation code were read to verify motif semantics and deterministic rendering. Each line reference is the 1-based JSONL row. The initial v1 responses were excluded.

I checked composition and reduced formula, space group and crystal system, cell-atom and independent-orbit counts, any Wyckoff labels stated, every included coordination number / neighbor-element mix / recognized motif / distance range, periodic graph claims, and original-MP energy-above-hull values and provenance. Structural number display may be rounded; the supplied ehull label must remain exact. Omissions were not counted as errors. Sentence counts use terminal `.?!` followed by whitespace and an uppercase letter or end of text, so decimals and `No. 123` do not create extra sentences.
